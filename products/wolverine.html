What's Inside

Four powerful peptides working together for comprehensive regeneration support.

GHK-Cu

"The Skin & Tissue Repair Peptide"

50 mg

A copper peptide that helps with repair and regeneration, especially in the skin.

  • Supports collagen and elastin production
  • Improves skin texture and firmness
  • Helps wound healing and scar repair
  • May support hair growth and hair quality
  • Has anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects

BPC-157

"The Soft Tissue & Gut Healer"

10 mg

A peptide derived from a natural protective protein found in the stomach.

  • Helps healing of tendons, ligaments, and muscles
  • Protects and repairs stomach and gut lining
  • Reduces inflammation in joints and soft tissues
  • May reduce pain from injuries

TB-500

"The Organizer of Repair"

10 mg

A synthetic peptide (from Thymosin Beta 4) that helps cells move and repair damaged areas.

  • Helps new blood vessels form in damaged areas
  • Speeds up repair of muscle, tendon, and ligament
  • Helps cells move into injured tissue
  • May reduce fibrosis and adhesions

KPV

"The Inflammation Fire Extinguisher"

10 mg

A small peptide (Lys-Pro-Val) known for its strong anti-inflammatory effects.

  • Strong systemic anti-inflammatory action
  • Helps calm the immune response
  • Possible benefits in gut inflammation
  • Can help reduce redness and swelling in skin
